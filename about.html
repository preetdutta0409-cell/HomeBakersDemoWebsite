Our story

A home kitchen.
A love for baking.

The Home Bakers Delhi grew from a mother-daughter baking journey in Greater Kailash and became a home bakery known for personalized cakes and bento-style creations.

The beginning

Mahima & Shraddha

The business was started by Mahima Wassan and her mother, Shraddha Wassan. Their baking journey began at home, with family and friends encouraging them to turn their love for baking into something more.

The story has been featured by food and lifestyle publications covering Delhi's home-baking scene.

The idea is simple: make something that feels personal, not mass-produced.
What matters

Personalization over repetition.

The website makes that difference visible: real cakes, real celebrations and an easy way to describe what you want.
